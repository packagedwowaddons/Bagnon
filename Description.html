Bagnon is a replacement of the bag interface, designed to help the player find items as quickly and easily as possible from anywhere.

It supports the inventory, bank and guild bank, which can be viewed at any time, from anywhere and from any character! It shows which of your characters have which items in tooltips. Advanced features such as customizable tabs, skins, and DataBroker support allow Bagnon to fit in any UI.

Want Bagnon to look more like the default interface instead? Try Bagnonium! You can also search the many available plugins for additional features.
